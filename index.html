Four tabs, one clear picture

Networks, channels, your connection, and a walk test.

Networks

Every access point in range with its name (hidden ones marked), BSSID, band (2.4, 5, 6 GHz), channel and width, signal strength with a quality bar, security (Open, WEP, WPA, WPA2, WPA3) and Wi-Fi generation (Wi-Fi 4, 5, 6, 6E, 7). Filter by band, sort by signal, name or channel.

Channels

A graph per band where each network is a curve over the channels it occupies, a best-channel recommendation for 2.4 GHz (1, 6, 11) and 5 GHz, and plain steps to change it on your router.

Connection

The live signal of the network you are on, updated every second, with link speed, frequency, channel, IP, gateway and a signal history graph.

Walk test

Name a room, hold still for 8 seconds, and get a Good, OK or Weak verdict. Compare spots to find dead zones.

How to use it

From a messy 2.4 GHz band to a clear plan.

  1. Allow access. Allow Location (and Nearby devices on Android 13+), and make sure Location and Wi-Fi are switched on. Android requires this to give any app scan results.
  2. Open Networks to see what is around you, then Channels to see which channel is least crowded and the suggested one for your router.
  3. Change the channel in your router settings, following the plain steps in the app, then check the Connection tab for the live signal.
  4. Walk through your home with the Walk test, one named spot at a time, and compare the verdicts to find weak areas.

Honest about the limits

Android decides how often an app may scan.

4 scans per 2 minutes

Android limits Wi-Fi scans to 4 per 2 minutes. Airscope shows when the last scan ran and uses cached results in between.

Location must be on

Without the Location permission and Location switched on, Android gives no scan results. Airscope shows an access screen until everything is ready.

A recommendation, not a promise

The best channel is computed from what your phone can hear right now. Your router and neighbors may still differ.

InternetOnly for the update check in Settings.

Requirements and install

Requirements

  • Wi-Fi switched on

Install
